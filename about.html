What we do

linkish.pub focuses on manual outreach, guest posts, link insertions and donor research. The goal is to connect websites with relevant publishing opportunities and make the process easier to manage.

How we work

Every campaign starts with the website, niche, GEO, target pages and link requirements. Websites can then be filtered by metrics such as organic traffic, DR/DA, relevance and overall quality.

Flexible niches

Different industries require different publisher criteria. We can work with a wide range of niches and adjust prospecting and outreach according to the project.

Transparent workflow

You can define the metrics, countries, topics, anchor requirements and budget before outreach begins. Placement details are provided after publication.
