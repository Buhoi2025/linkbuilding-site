Complete Guest Posting Guide

Guest posting can be part of an outreach campaign when the publisher, content and link are genuinely relevant to the audience.

What is guest posting?

Guest posting means creating an article for publication on another website. When appropriate, the article can contain a contextual link to a relevant resource on your website.

How to select publishers

Look at topical relevance, organic visibility, audience location, publishing consistency, content quality and the site's outbound linking patterns. Avoid choosing publishers solely because they advertise a high authority metric.

Prepare useful content

The article should answer a real question for the publisher's audience. Use clear headings, examples, original explanations and natural internal and external references.

Outreach matters

A personalized message should quickly explain why your proposed topic fits the website. Avoid sending the same generic pitch to every publisher.

Before publication

Confirm the target URL, anchor, placement location, content requirements, price, publishing timeline and any editorial restrictions.
